Enabling Google Gemini Live AI (Optional)

By default, Kalamkari Connect runs with a built-in Offline Knowledge Engine pre-programmed with Srikalahasti & Machilipatnam craft traditions.

To switch from Demo Engine to Live Google Gemini API:

  1. Get a free Google Gemini API key from Google AI Studio.
  2. Open the .env file in the project directory.
  3. Add your key: GEMINI_API_KEY=your_actual_key_here
  4. Restart Flask! The app will automatically detect your key and switch modes.
