About Kalamkari Connect

An AI-powered digital initiative bridging the centuries-old heritage of Indian hand-painted textiles with sustainable, fair-trade e-commerce.

Preserving Living Heritage

Kalamkari (from Persian qalam meaning pen, and kari meaning craft) is a celebrated GI-tagged Indian textile art form rooted in Andhra Pradesh. Flourishing in Srikalahasti and Machilipatnam, it utilizes natural dyes derived from madder roots, myrobalan nuts, and fermented iron jaggery water across a rigorous 17-step organic process.

Despite its cultural grandeur, hereditary artisans frequently face exploitation from intermediaries and lack direct market access. Kalamkari Connect empowers these master craftspeople through direct-to-consumer sales, transparent pricing, and multilingual AI assistance.

Authentic pen Kalamkari motif depicting the celestial blue peacock, natural vegetable dyes on unbleached cotton.

Direct Artisan Support

100% of product proceeds directly reach hereditary artisan families with fair compensation and no middlemen.

Authenticity & GI Tag

Every listed craft is curated for genuine Geographical Indication provenance from Srikalahasti or Machilipatnam.

AI-Powered Storytelling

Artisans utilize Kalamkari Mitra AI to generate captivating product descriptions, heritage stories, and social captions.
